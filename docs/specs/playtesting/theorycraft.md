# Theory crafting studies

The theory tools work with explicit hypothetical Magic positions and declared models. An archived replay can supply seat-authorized evidence only once the encounter ends. Assumptions about the other player come from public matchup hypotheses or data separately authorized for that purpose. A favorable branch found under such an assumption is scoped to it.

- **PM-THEORY-1** Monte Carlo preparation is reproducible from the model version, seed, repetitions and inputs. Declarative queries can measure card and tag presence, lands, sources, true mana castability, and a supplied casting line. Reports include the observed numerator, denominator, interval, assumptions and model limits.
- **PM-THEORY-2** One sample gives alternative policies common draws and opening hand candidates. The policies see only cards already in their hand or visible zones at the decision. A policy with a London mulligan sees seven current cards, bottoms its required number on keep, observes the first-turn play or draw rule, and cannot use sampled future draws in its keep or mana decisions.
- **PM-THEORY-3** The mana model uses one land drop per turn, untaps existing lands, honors explicitly tapped entry and assigns distinct usable sources to every repeated colored, colorless and generic mana symbol. A land with two possible colors still pays only one mana. Unsupported costs or dependencies are excluded from the claim.
- **PM-THEORY-4** A conditional study preserves declared known slots and randomizes unobserved cards without replacement. An explicit partial shuffle moves only the cards in its range. Response hypotheses give sensitivity across named distributions. The tool distinguishes executing an input line from a policy's estimated win rate.
- **PM-THEORY-5** A tactical search on a finite legal decision tree can prove a forced line only when all relevant rival responses are covered within the horizon and budget. Sampled or unknown outcomes, partial enumeration, a budget stop and an engine-witnessed line are distinct. Chance nodes with specified finite distributions can contribute expected outcomes.
- **PM-THEORY-6** A history client can link a study result to an authorized persisted review annotation. A conjecture about the opponent is separate from a confirmed public reply and from hindsight. Study artifacts stay within their authorized owner and replay context.

The study expression contract adapts the public-card and tag query patterns of the local Forger Magic Theorycraft app. Its `prepare_mulligan_simulation`, `query_mulligan_strategy`, `validate_mulligan_rule_expression`, and `can_cast_tag_by_turn` are reference points. The original presence-of-color estimate is not the mana assignment algorithm for this implementation.
